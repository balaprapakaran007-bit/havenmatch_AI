# HavenMatch AI Matching & Property Engine

This skill manages the AI lifestyle matching, property lifecycle, buyer/owner feeds, inquiries, visits, and MongoDB Atlas database operations for HavenMatch AI.

## Workflow Capabilities
- **Two-Sided Ingress**: Routes buyer matching, seller listings, compatible buyers, shortlists, interests, visits, connections, and reviews.
- **Dynamic AI Matching**: Evaluates buyer budget, BHK, city/locality, and lifestyle signals (noise, water supply, pet-friendly) against live MongoDB properties.
- **Workflow Asset**: The complete 36-node visual workflow is exported at `havenmatch_workflow.json` in the workspace root.
